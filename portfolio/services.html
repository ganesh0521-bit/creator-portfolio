Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, rights, and delivery.

Do you travel for international shoots?

Yes! Over 60% of our commercial and documentary projects are filmed internationally. We hold active travel visas, ATA Carnets for seamless international customs clearance of cinema gear, and drone pilot licenses.

What are the commercial usage rights included?

All commercial packages include perpetual, worldwide digital and web usage rights across website, YouTube, social channels, and digital ads. Broadcast TV / theatrical buyouts are available on request.

How does payment and booking deposit work?

We operate on a standard 50% deposit upon contract signing to lock in production dates on the studio calendar, with the remaining 50% due upon delivery of final approved watermarked masters.
